Our Services & Treatments

Comprehensive non-surgical medical care tailored to your needs

Neurology Treatments

Expert neurological care for brain, nerve & spine conditions

Neurology Consultation

Expert diagnosis and management of neurological disorders including migraines, epilepsy, and neuropathy. Our neurologists use advanced diagnostic tools to provide accurate assessments and personalized treatment plans.

EEG & Nerve Studies

Non-invasive diagnostic testing for brain activity and peripheral nerve function. Our advanced EEG and nerve conduction studies help detect and monitor neurological conditions accurately.

Vertigo & Balance Therapy

Specialized treatment for dizziness, BPPV, and vestibular rehabilitation programs. Our balance therapy programs are designed to restore stability and confidence in daily activities.

Headache & Pain Management

Comprehensive non-surgical approaches to chronic headaches and neurological pain. Our multidisciplinary team creates customized pain management plans for lasting relief.

Parkinson's Disease Management

Comprehensive care for tremors, rigidity and movement disorders. Our neurologists design medication optimization and therapy programs to improve mobility and quality of life.

Multiple Sclerosis Care

Specialized treatment plans for MS symptom management. We offer personalized therapies to slow progression and improve daily functioning for patients at every stage.

Sleep Disorder Treatment

Diagnosis and non-surgical management of sleep disorders including insomnia and parasomnia. We conduct advanced sleep studies and provide targeted therapy for restful sleep.

Cognitive Assessment

Evaluation of memory, attention and overall brain function. Our advanced neuropsychological testing enables early detection and intervention for cognitive decline.

Spine & Back Pain Therapy

Non-surgical solutions for chronic spinal and nerve pain. We offer targeted nerve blocks, physiotherapy and rehabilitation programs for lasting spinal relief.

ENT Treatments

Specialized ear, nose & throat care for all ages

ENT Examination

Complete ear, nose, and throat assessments including hearing tests and allergy management. We offer thorough evaluations using state-of-the-art equipment for precise diagnosis.

Sinus & Allergy Care

Medical management of chronic sinusitis, allergic rhinitis, and respiratory conditions. We provide comprehensive allergy testing and personalized treatment strategies for long-term relief.

Preventive Health Screening

Regular health check-ups, lifestyle counseling, and chronic disease monitoring. Stay ahead of potential health issues with our comprehensive preventive care programs.

Sleep & Snoring Evaluation

Assessment and non-invasive management of sleep apnea and snoring disorders. We help you achieve better sleep quality through comprehensive evaluation and targeted therapies.

Tinnitus Treatment

Specialized care for persistent ringing and buzzing in the ears. Our sound therapy and advanced treatments help reduce tinnitus severity and improve quality of life.

Deviated Septum Correction

Non-surgical and minimally invasive septoplasty options to restore proper nasal airflow. Breathe easier with expert nasal airway correction procedures.

Ear Infection Treatment

Expert care for acute and chronic ear infections in both children and adults. Targeted antibiotics and drainage procedures ensure fast and effective recovery.

Thyroid & Neck Evaluation

Comprehensive screening for thyroid and neck masses using advanced imaging and biopsy techniques. Early detection and accurate diagnosis for peace of mind.

Speech & Swallowing Therapy

Rehabilitation for speech disorders and dysphagia. Personalized therapy programs restore clear communication and safe swallowing for patients of all ages.
